NIGHT VISION RADAR

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 18, 2026

Privacy First

Night Vision Radar is designed to process LiDAR, depth, and camera information directly on the user's device. The app does not require an account or user registration.

Information We Collect

Night Vision Radar does not collect, store, sell, or share personal information with the developer.

The app does not require users to create an account and does not require users to provide their name, email address, phone number, location, or other personal information.

Camera

Night Vision Radar requires access to the iPhone camera to provide its real-time visualization features.

Camera information is processed on the device for the operation of the app and is not transmitted to the developer.

LiDAR and Depth Data

On compatible devices, Night Vision Radar uses Apple's LiDAR and related depth-sensing capabilities to visualize depth, distance, and spatial geometry.

LiDAR and depth information used by the app is processed locally on the device and is not transmitted to the developer.

Photo Library

Night Vision Radar may request permission to add images to the user's Photo Library when the user chooses to save a capture.

This permission is used only to save captures requested by the user. The app does not upload the user's Photo Library to the developer.

Location Data

Night Vision Radar does not request or collect the user's location.

Tracking and Advertising

Night Vision Radar does not use user information for advertising or cross-app tracking.

Third-Party Services

Night Vision Radar does not intentionally transmit personal information to third-party advertising or analytics services.

Children's Privacy

Night Vision Radar does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data Security

The app is designed so that its core camera, LiDAR, and depth processing occurs locally on the user's device.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the app's features or data practices change. Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised effective date.
