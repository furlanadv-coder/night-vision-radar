Device Compatibility

Night Vision Radar requires an iPhone equipped with a LiDAR sensor for its LiDAR-based features.

Camera and Photo Library

Camera access is used to provide the app's real-time visualization. Photo Library permission is requested only when necessary to save captures created by the user.

Troubleshooting

If LiDAR visualization is unavailable, verify that you are using a LiDAR-compatible device and that Camera permission has been granted to Night Vision Radar.

If captures are not being saved, verify that Night Vision Radar has permission to add photos to your Photo Library.

Important Notice

Night Vision Radar is intended for visual exploration, LiDAR experimentation and spatial visualization. It is not intended to be used as a professional measurement, navigation, surveillance or safety device.
